## Phase 2 — display + touch (bench, 2026-09-24)
- Vendor schematic (`2.8inch_SPI_Module_MSP2833_MSP2834_Schematic.pdf`, 2022-12-02) read: LED pin →
  0 R → BSS138 gate, 10 K pull-up to 3.3 V, drain → LEDK via 2 R: logic/PWM input, floating = ON.
  Touch CTP_SDA/SCL: BSS138 shifters with 10 K pull-ups to **module VCC** on the header side → a
  5 V bus on GPIO9/40 when VCC = 5 V (0.1 mA clamp; out of spec, not destructive). TFT signals go
  through a 74LVC245 (3.3 V); SDO straight from the panel.
- Host gate PASS: `[Display] ILI9341 RDID4 = 0x9341 (OK)`, `[Touch] FT6336 initialized`,
  `post LCD=OK(id 0x9341) LED=OK Buzzer=OK RFID=FAIL(0x00) Touch=OK LoRa=OK`; bonus
  `[LoRa] SX1262 up (AS923, TCXO 1.8V, DIO2 RF switch)` (no TX). Operator visual + touch pending.
- PlatformIO's `pio run -t upload` failed twice ("No serial data received") while the app was
  running; direct esptool with `--connect-attempts 30` succeeded both times.
